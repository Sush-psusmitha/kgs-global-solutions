Our AI Capabilities for Insurance

Make Routine Insurance Work Move Faster

KGS uses AI agents and insurance workflow automation to:

  • Retrieve policy information across connected systems
  • Coordinate recurring claims workflows
  • Flag exceptions requiring human review
  • Route defined process actions

Routine tasks move faster, giving claims professionals and underwriters greater capacity while retaining human oversight for complex decisions, approvals, and insurance judgment.

Turn Insurance Data into Earlier Performance Insight

We leverage insurance analytics services across claims, policyholder, and underwriting data to:

  • Identify claims and policy patterns
  • Track processing cycle times
  • Detect anomalies requiring investigation
  • Inform risk and performance decisions

Teams gain clearer visibility into processing trends, emerging risks, potential leakage, and bottlenecks requiring timely action across the insurance lifecycle.

Take Repetitive Work Out of the Policy Lifecycle

Structured insurance workflows can create significant administrative effort at scale. KGS applies automation to:

  • Standardize recurring process steps
  • Automate rule-based validations
  • Route exceptions to the appropriate teams
  • Connect activities across existing insurance systems

This improves consistency and turnaround while giving experienced insurance professionals more capacity for risk, judgment, and customer outcomes.
