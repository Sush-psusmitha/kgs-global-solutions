Our Success Journey

Everyone has a story.
Here is ours.

2000

From One Client to a Growing Vision

KGS was founded with 6 employees and 1 client, beginning with customer support for a global apparel brand. That first mandate established a principle that continues to shape KGS: earn trust through hard work, then grow.

2005

Building a Global Delivery Presence

Our company grew to 150+ employees and established a genuine global delivery presence within five years of starting operations with a single client.

2007

Taking on Challenges Others Avoided

Entered Canadian customs brokerage, a technically demanding specialty most providers avoided. Our strong execution of the project even led us to becoming a business school case study.

2008

Four New Service Lines in One Year

Launched four new service lines in a single year: order management, healthcare BPO, finance and accounting, and ecommerce support. Each matured into core strengths within four years. The growth was featured in a leading global business publication.

2012

A Decade of Operational Discipline

Crossed 1,500 employees and earned two major quality certifications, covering process standards and information security, formalizing the operational discipline built over a decade.

2014

Building Expertise Within

Launched a training and certification division offering 28+ globally recognized courses. We ranked among the top 10 emerging BPOs in India the same year.

2017

AI and Automation Enter the Picture

Launched a dedicated AI and automation practice, way ahead of its adoption across various industries. International research analysts recognized KGS as a vendor in freight and payments technology that very same year.

2018

Scaling Up in Delivery Capacity

KGS crossed 2,500 employees in a continued effort to expand delivery capacity and global operations. The growing team strengthened our ability to support more clients and take on more complex work.

2020

From BPO to Business Transformation Company

Repositioned from a BPO to a business process transformation company, backed by a major enterprise software partnership and a move into a new, technology-first delivery facility.

2022

Shipping Out Our First AI Product

Became GDPR compliant, secured two state government contracts, and shipped the first enterprise AI SaaS product, purpose-built for the education sector. This was our shift from AI-enabled services to AI products.

2023

Applying Generative AI to Real Operations

We launched a full generative AI services line, applying large language models to real operational work, with intelligent document processing, AI-assisted customer support, and content generation at scale.

2025

From AI That Assists to AI That Acts

Launched an agentic AI platform, moving beyond AI that assists to AI that acts. Agents now handle repeatable execution end to end, while people handle judgment calls, exceptions, and client relationships.

2026

Our GCC Model Takes Shape

Formalized the model that had been in the making for years: a Global Capability Center built to the standard clients actually need, not the offshore-cost-center version the term usually implies. Every function delivery, quality, technology, and account leadership, operates inside one accountable center, not spread across vendors. This is the year the GCC model stopped being an internal operating philosophy and became the pitch.
