International trade demands accuracy at every stage. KGS manages customs documentation, tariff classification, and duty calculations through experienced customs brokers and global trade compliance expertise, enabling faster customs clearance, fewer disruptions, and smoother cross-border trade.

What we do

Keep Your Shipments Compliant with Our Customs Brokerage Solutions

Get complete customs brokerage support across documentation, classification, duty calculation, and compliance, backed by proven expertise and structured delivery practices that keep cross-border shipments moving.

Customs Documentation

Our team prepares accurate customs documentation and filing records that support timely clearance and reduce your documentation errors.

HS Tariff Classification

At KGS, you get reliable HS code classification services that assign the correct tariff codes, supporting compliant declarations and accurate duty assessment.

Duty & Tax Calculation

We calculate duties, taxes, and applicable charges with precision, giving you greater visibility into landed costs and regulatory obligations.

Trade Compliance

Our domain-certified team manages global trade compliance requirements through regulatory reviews, screening, and filing practices that support compliant cross-border trade.

Import & Export Processing

Our import customs clearance and export customs clearance specialists manage filings from submission through release to keep freight moving efficiently.

Customs Valuation

KGS provides you with accurate customs valuation backed by complete documentation and accepted valuation methods that withstand regulatory review.

FTA & Duty Optimization

We review trade agreements and duty structures to identify legitimate savings while maintaining full compliance with applicable regulations.

Denied Party Screening

Our screening process checks transactions against restricted party lists before shipment, strengthening compliance across international trade.
