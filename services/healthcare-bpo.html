What we do

Healthcare Solutions That Keep Your Care and Revenue Moving

Managing the revenue cycle demands accuracy, speed, and compliance. KGS provides revenue cycle management services backed by experienced specialists, delivering dependable support across billing, coding, claims, and other critical functions.

Medical Billing

Through medical billing outsourcing, specialists submit accurate claims, resolve discrepancies, and maintain consistent reimbursement across the revenue cycle.

Medical Coding

Certified coders at KGS assign ICD-10 and CPT codes with precision, supporting compliant documentation and dependable medical coding services.

Claims Management

Advanced tools streamline healthcare claims processing from submission through resolution, accelerating claim progress while reducing outstanding balances.

Prior Authorization

Get faster authorization workflows backed by experienced specialists who manage documentation, payer communication, and approvals with accuracy.

Accounts Receivable Management

Our team of skilled, experienced specialists follows up on outstanding receivables, identifies payment gaps, and drives collections to strengthen financial performance.

Denial Management & Appeals

We analyze denial trends, manage timely appeals, and address recurring claim issues through focused healthcare support services and corrective actions.

Eligibility Verification

Our expert team verifies insurance eligibility, patient benefits, and registration details before services are delivered, improving your billing accuracy from the start.

Payment Posting & Reconciliation

Accurate payment posting and reconciliation identify underpayments quickly while maintaining complete financial records and reporting.

Practical AI Application for Revenue Cycle Excellence

KGS applies AI where it delivers measurable business value. Revenue cycle management services benefit from predictive analytics, intelligent dashboards, Generative AI, and healthcare workflow automation, which improve claim quality, reduce manual effort, and strengthen financial visibility.
