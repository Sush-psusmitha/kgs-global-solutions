What we do

Legal Process Solutions

KGS covers legal process outsourcing services across document review, research, contracts, litigation, and compliance with consistent accuracy and precision.

Document Review

Our legal reviewers analyze, classify, and code large document sets with speed and precision, supporting efficient discovery while protecting privileged information.

Legal Research

We conduct case law, statutory, and precedent research that gives legal teams reliable analysis for litigation, advisory work, and commercial matters.

Contract Management

We handle drafting, review, abstraction, and contract lifecycle management services with consistent accuracy and attention to detail.

Litigation Support

KGS manages discovery materials, exhibits, chronologies, and trial documentation with disciplined execution and strict adherence to legal deadlines.

Legal Transcription

Get accurately transcribed hearings, depositions, and legal proceedings delivered in the required format and within agreed turnaround times.

Compliance Support

Our specialists prepare regulatory documentation and compliance records that support governance requirements across jurisdictions.

IP & Patent Support

We support intellectual property portfolios through patent research, drafting assistance, filing preparation, and documentation management.

Legal Data Entry

At KGS, we digitize, index, and structure legal records for faster retrieval, stronger document control, and reliable case management.
